Your estimate–

Lock PESOLAR to share a board's reward pool in proportion to what you lock. Locked PESOLAR can't be sent until the board's payout date. Unlocking early frees it but gives up your share. A board only takes new PESOLAR during the half-month it starts in (days 1-15 or 16-end), so it can't be joined at the last minute. You can have one lock in each board, but the same PESOLAR can't be locked in two boards. Once the payout date passes, claim your share within a year (unlocking then claims it for you).

Get points

Points can't be withdrawn back to Wax, but you can sell them to other players for PESOLAR. A 10% fee on the points you list is burned when you sell. Points are used to capture lands: if you hold a land for 24 hours, you get back about 40-90% of your bid (the higher the bid, the fewer the points you get back). If someone outbids you, you get your bid back plus at least 4 points. What the lands earn is PESOLAR, which you can keep, send, sell or lock. Having more points lets you capture more lands and earn more PESOLAR.
